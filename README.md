## About Me

I'm a Biostatistician and technology enthusiast interested in building practical solutions at the intersection of **data, software, systems, automation, and artificial intelligence**.

My background in biostatistics gives me a strong foundation in statistical thinking, research, and problem solving, while my technical work has expanded into application development, business intelligence, workflow automation, and systems analysis.

I enjoy taking a problem from an initial idea, understanding how the pieces fit together, building a solution, and turning it into something people can actually use.

### Areas I Work In

* **Software Development** — Building applications, interactive tools, and web-based solutions
* **Systems & Process Automation** — Designing workflows that reduce repetitive manual processes
* **Data & Business Intelligence** — Data analysis, dashboards, reporting, and decision-support tools
* **Biostatistics & Research** — Statistical modelling, survival analysis, and healthcare research
* **Artificial Intelligence** — Exploring practical applications of AI and machine learning
* **Data Engineering Concepts** — Working with databases, SQL, data pipelines, and structured data
* **Technical Documentation** — Translating technical systems and workflows into clear documentation

## Projects

### Healthcare Claims Dashboard

**Power BI | DAX | Data Modelling**

An interactive dashboard designed to monitor healthcare claims and provide a structured view of claims activity, trends, and key performance indicators.

---

### Customer Feedback Analyzer

**Python | NLP | Data Processing**

A Python-based application that processes customer feedback and uses natural language processing techniques to identify patterns and extract useful insights from unstructured text.

---

### Motor Insurance Shiny Application

**R | Shiny | Statistical Computing**

An interactive R Shiny application for exploring and analysing motor insurance data through a user-friendly interface.

---

### Antenatal Care Survival Analysis

**R | Survival Analysis | Biostatistics**

A research project using Kenya Demographic and Health Survey data to investigate time to first antenatal care attendance.

The project involved data cleaning, survival analysis, Kaplan-Meier estimation, Cox proportional hazards assessment, and accelerated failure time modelling.

---

### Expenditure Tracker

**Python | Streamlit | Data Management**

A lightweight application for recording and monitoring personal expenditure, with a focus on simple data entry, categorisation, and financial summaries.
